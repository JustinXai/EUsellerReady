## Phase 2 Content Roadmap

### EAA - European Accessibility Act
- **Scope:** EAA applies from June 2025; may affect ecommerce stores and digital services
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/eaa-compliance-for-ecommerce/`
  - `/eaa-for-shopify/`
  - `/eaa-for-amazon-sellers/`
- **Key topics:** Website accessibility requirements, self-assessment checklist, provider matching

### PPWR - Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation
- **Scope:** New EU regulation replacing current EPR framework; more stringent requirements
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/ppwr-compliance-guide/`
  - `/ppwr-for-shopify/`
- **Key topics:** Recyclability requirements, packaging reduction targets, registration

### CE Marking
- **Scope:** CE marking for electronics, toys, cosmetics, machinery sold in EU
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/ce-marking-for-ecommerce/`
  - `/ce-marking-checklist/`
- **Key topics:** When CE marking applies, self-assessment vs. notified body, documentation

### WEEE - Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment
- **Scope:** Registration for sellers of electronics in EU
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/weee-compliance-for-shopify/`
- **Key topics:** WEEE producer obligations, registration by country, recycling fees

### Batteries Directive
- **Scope:** EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/battery-compliance-for-ecommerce/`
- **Key topics:** Battery categories, labeling, recycling, safety

### Cosmetics Regulation (EU)
- **Scope:** EU Cosmetics Regulation 1223/2009
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/cosmetics-eu-compliance/`
- **Key topics:** Responsible Person, safety assessment, ingredient labeling, CPNP notification

### Toy Safety Directive
- **Scope:** Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC (GPSR may supersede)
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/toy-safety-for-ecommerce/`
- **Key topics:** EN71 testing, CE marking, age warnings, choking hazards

### DPP - Digital Product Passport
- **Scope:** EU regulation under Green Deal; phased implementation
- **Pages to create:**
  - `/dpp-readiness-guide/`
- **Key topics:** Which products need DPP, data requirements, timeline
